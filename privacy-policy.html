Privacy Policy for Tuck & Tales

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Tuck & Tales is a bedtime storybook app made for children. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. The short version: Tuck & Tales does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone, including children.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, request, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • Photographs, audio, video, or any content created by the user
  • Location data of any kind
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Advertising identifiers or any persistent device identifiers
  • Analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, or behavioural data

The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. There is no way for a child or a parent to enter personal information into Tuck & Tales.

Information stored on your device

When you mark a story as a favourite, the app saves that story's identifier in your device's local app storage so your favourites are still there the next time you open the app. This information:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not transmitted to us or to anyone else;
  • contains no personal information — only which stories were marked; and
  • is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet access

Tuck & Tales requests internet permission for one purpose only: to download the story illustrations that accompany each story. These images are hosted on Cloudinary, a content delivery service. Downloaded illustrations are cached on your device so they load quickly and work offline afterwards.

These are ordinary one-way image downloads. The app sends no personal information, no identifiers, and no information about the reader as part of these requests.

Advertising and third-party services

Tuck & Tales contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party advertising networks, and no analytics or tracking software of any kind. No third-party SDK collects information through this app. We do not sell, rent, or share information with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

Tuck & Tales is intentionally designed for children and is built to be safe for them to use. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it collects none from children either.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including provisions concerning children, and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and there is no mechanism in the app by which such information could be collected. Because no personal information is collected, verifiable parental consent is not required, and there is no personal data for a parent to review or request deletion of.

Content

All stories and illustrations in Tuck & Tales are selected by us and included in the app. There is no user-generated content, no chat, no social features, no comments, and no way for users to communicate with each other or with strangers. The app contains no external links or web browser.

Security

Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data that could be exposed in a data breach. Favourites are stored using the standard app-private storage provided by the operating system.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. If a future version of the app ever collects any information, we will update this policy before releasing it.
